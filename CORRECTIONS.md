# Corrections

## 2026-10-28 — errata to state-contingent-comparability 3.0.0-candidate (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23392424)

The archived PDF, the manuscript source, the Zenodo deposit and the tag `v3.0.0-candidate` are unchanged. These errata were confirmed by the Evidence Press archive audit and are also shown at <https://evidencepress.org/releases/state-contingent-comparability/>.

1. Corrected a notation clash in §5, equation (12). The archived text writes each new cash flow on a regime as c_it(x) = a_it + b_itᵀx, but §3.1 defines a_it as creditor i’s old contractual service. Read literally, (12) would force every new cash flow’s intercept to equal the old service, which the paper’s own examples (§9 and §12.4) do not satisfy. The intercept needs its own symbol, for example c_it(x) = e_it + b_itᵀx.

   Only the notation is affected; the verification method and the worked examples use a free intercept as intended. No theorem, certificate, computation or stated result is affected.

2. Corrected a cross-reference in §8, in the paragraph after Theorem 8.1. The archived text says “If the base passes, all right-hand sides in (29) are nonnegative”. Inequality (29) is a single row; the statement concerns all three rows, so it should read “all right-hand sides in (29)–(31) are nonnegative”. A passing base gives B_V − V_b ≥ 0, B_S − S_b ≥ 0 and, from its duration pass, M_b − d∗V_b ≥ 0.

   Only the cross-reference is affected. No theorem, certificate, computation or stated result is affected.
