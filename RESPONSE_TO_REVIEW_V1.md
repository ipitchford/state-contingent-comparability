The revision also tightens an unbounded edge case in the information theorem: observation classes must be nonempty, and the supremum of required payment floors must be finite. Otherwise an extended-real comparison `infinity <= infinity` could incorrectly suggest that a finite payment exists. This does not change any submitted finite example. Additional measurability and contractual expressibility restrictions are explicitly outside the elementary interval intersection unless separately imposed.

The strongest unresolved criticism is significance: the method is an application-focused synthesis of established tools, and synthetic examples alone do not demonstrate an operational improvement in a real restructuring. The revised manuscript acknowledges this directly. Computational repairs and expanded presentation do not by themselves establish a higher research-assessment rating.
