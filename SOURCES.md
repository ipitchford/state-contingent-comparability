# Sources and search coverage

**Search and observation dates: 13–15 October 2026.** The dated versions below are the evidential basis, not a claim about later revisions. The manuscript contains 14 reference entries representing 13 distinct works (the Asonuma working paper and journal version are one research lineage).

## Search method

The search combined exact-title lookup, mechanism queries, and reference following. It covered institutional comparability definitions; state-contingent instruments, caps and trigger design; old/new present-value normalisation and duration; adjustable robust optimisation and information sets; and exact polynomial/quadratic verification. Discovery used live web search, original institutional sites, author-hosted full texts, arXiv and publisher/DOI records. A final 15 October search targeted the strict endpoint mechanism and the nonproportional oblique-trigger extension. Semantic Scholar was attempted twice but returned HTTP 429 without authentication. That route contributed no claimed coverage. No citation-count filter or paid literature service was used.

Representative queries were: `sovereign debt comparability treatment duration state contingent payout caps exact robust optimization`; `duration cash flow proportional reduction covariance payment timing Macaulay duration`; `quadratic programming polytope stationary points faces exact global minimum enumeration`; `comparability of treatment Gelpern`; `sovereign debt duration haircuts Zettelmeyer`; `comparability duration state-contingent debt exact`; `state contingent sovereign debt oblique trigger implementable cap nonanticipativity`; `componentwise smaller cash flows duration comparison counterexample`; and exact titles of the works below. Reference following added the 2025 IMF stocktaking and the 2017 restructuring valuation paper.

Inclusion required direct relevance to a load-bearing definition, methodological antecedent, interpretation or contextual boundary. Search results about unrelated bond-risk applications, general political commentary or unrelated 2026 macroeconomic events were excluded. Earlier and later versions were distinguished. This is a structured, extensive review, not an exhaustive database export, PRISMA review or proof of priority. No invented screening counts or coverage percentage are supplied.

## Closest-work conclusions

Caps and floors, discounted-duration identities, polynomial denominator clearing and nonanticipative decisions are established tools. The revision does not offer them as new general discoveries. The additional contribution is the explicit sovereign-comparability formulation, a strict endpoint-passing interior failure, a reusable payment-dominance diagnostic, and exact application-specific design examples including a nonproportional family in which an oblique trigger changes both the sharp cap and the binding metric.

The search did not settle the priority of the exact counterexample or combined formulation. A negative result within this search cannot establish global novelty. The strongest substantive limitation remains the absence of an authenticated operational case study and practitioner validation of an acceptance region.
